Common questions about our land-funding approach

Straightforward answers about how buyer ownership rights and equity stay protected.

Who owns the home?

The buyer holds sole legal title to the home structure, retaining 100% of home equity and structural appreciation.

Can the land be purchased later?

Yes. Homeowners can purchase the land at any time without buyout penalties, using transparent, public market data to determine pricing.

What happens when I want to sell?

You sell your home on the open market just like any standard property. The new buyer can assume the land agreement or buy the house and land together. You keep all house profits.
