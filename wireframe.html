The Two-Step Approach: Separate the house from the land.

The biggest hurdle to buying a home is not the house itself—it is the dirt underneath it. Land drives up the total purchase price, inflates your monthly mortgage payment, and demands a massive pile of cash for a down payment. Two-Step Homeownership solves this by removing the land from your mortgage equation.

The standard way to buy a home with less cash.

You buy the physical house with a standard mortgage, and Jubilee covers the land. By financing only the structure, your required down payment drops by up to 75%—getting you into a single-family home on your current income.

Your name. Your deed. Your profit.

Even though you are only financing the structure, you have total control. Your name goes on the county deed. You can paint the walls, remodel the kitchen, and get a dog. When you are ready to move, you sell the home on the open market and keep the profit.

What's the catch? (How Jubilee makes money)

Transparency builds trust, so let's look at the exact math. Jubilee makes money in two straightforward ways, and neither involves hidden costs:

  • The Monthly Land Payment: While you pay your standard mortgage to your bank for the house, you make a low, predictable Monthly Land Payment to Jubilee. Even with this combined structure, your total monthly housing cost is still substantially lower than a traditional mortgage.
  • Land Appreciation: When you eventually sell the home, or if you choose to buy the land from us later, Jubilee benefits from the natural market growth of the land.

The bottom line: You keep the profit on the house. We keep the profit on the land.

The real cost of waiting for the "perfect time."

If your rent is $3,200 a month, waiting one more year to buy costs you $38,400. That money builds zero equity and offers zero tax benefits. Waiting for interest rates to drop often means you will just pay a higher purchase price when bidding wars start. Two-Step allows you to stop paying a landlord and start building your own net worth today.

Buy the land whenever you are ready.

You are never forced to buy the land. There are no surprise deadlines and no balloon payments. You can buy the land in year 3, year 10, or never. If you do decide to buy it, the price is based on standard public housing data, not a hidden Jubilee fee.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I have to share my equity when I sell?

No. You keep the profit from the house. Every dollar of principal you pay down and every dollar of market appreciation on the house belongs to you.

Can I use my own real estate agent?

Yes. Your agent represents you, helps you write a standard offer, and earns their full normal commission at closing.

What kind of homes can I buy?

You can buy regular single-family homes on the open market. You are not restricted to a small list of company-owned properties.
