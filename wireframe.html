Traditional Mortgage

Requires financing the house and land together. Demands massive upfront cash, high monthly debt ratios, and locks up liquid capital in low-yield land equity.

Jubilee's Model

You finance the house structure (~$500,000) while Jubilee funds the land (~$400,000). Your monthly cost includes your mortgage and fixed land lease. You keep one hundred percent of the home equity.

*Jubilee's homeownership model means you buy and own the home; Jubilee owns the land under it and you lease it for up to ninety-nine years. Your monthly housing cost includes both your mortgage payment and your land lease payment—the figures above include both. You keep 100% of the equity in the home itself. Appreciation on the land is shared under the terms of your lease, and you have the option to buy the land at any time. Example figures only, your terms depend on the home, your credit and your market.
