How Jubilee Works: The Complete Breakdown

Everything you need to know about our homeownership model, structured in plain English.

01 Who

Built for aspiring homeowners and capital-conscious buyers who want single-family real estate without draining their liquid savings.

02 What

A homeownership model that separates the house structure from the land underneath it, dramatically lowering upfront cash requirements.

03 When

Applied at closing. You secure your mortgage for the house while Jubilee simultaneously acquires and funds the underlying land.

04 Where

Available on standard single-family residential properties and neighborhoods across your target market.

05 How

Your name goes on the county title deed as the legal owner of the structure. You pay a predictable monthly land lease alongside your mortgage.

06 Why

To eliminate the inflated cost of dirt, giving families affordable access to great school districts and investors freedom to keep cash liquid.

Common questions about separating house and land

Clear, straightforward answers about our legal structure, title deeds, and your ownership rights.

Who owns the home?

You do. Your name is recorded on the standard county title deed as the legal owner of the structure. You retain 100% of the home equity and structural appreciation.

Can I buy the land later?

Yes. You can choose to buy the land from Jubilee at any time on your own timeline with zero buyout penalties, using objective, public housing appreciation data for transparent pricing.

What happens when I want to sell?

You sell your home on the open market just like any standard property. The new buyer can assume the land agreement or buy the house and land together. You keep all house profits.

Can I work with my own agent?

Yes. We work directly with your licensed real estate agent. You get the home you want, and your agent earns their full traditional buyer-broker commission at closing.
