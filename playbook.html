Module 4 — How Jubilee Works (The Framework)

How Jubilee Works: The Complete Breakdown

Everything to know about the land-funding model, structured clearly in plain English.

01
Who

Built for aspiring homeowners and cash-conscious buyers who want single-family real estate without draining their liquid savings.

02
What

An approach that separates the house structure from the land underneath it, dramatically lowering upfront cash requirements.

03
When

Applied at closing. You secure your mortgage for the house while Jubilee simultaneously acquires and funds the underlying land.

04
Where

Available on standard single-family residential properties and neighborhoods across where you're looking to buy.

05
How

Homeowners hold legal title to the home structure, paying a predictable monthly land agreement fee alongside standard mortgage payments.

06
Why

To eliminate the inflated cost of land, giving families affordable access to great school districts and buyers freedom to keep cash liquid.

Module 5 — Trust & Normalcy Pillars

Standard home purchase financing. Completely normal real estate.

Full Homeownership

You are the sole, legal owner of the home, exactly the same as a traditional purchase.

Traditional Lenders

Your Own Agent

Module 7 — Mechanism FAQ

Common questions about our land-funding approach

Straightforward answers about how buyer ownership rights and equity stay protected.

Q: Who owns the home?

A: The buyer holds sole legal title to the home structure, retaining 100% of home equity and structural appreciation.

Q: Can the land be purchased later?

A: Yes. Homeowners can purchase the land from Jubilee at any time without buyout penalties, using transparent, public market data to determine pricing.

Q: What happens when I want to sell?

A: You sell your home on the open market just like any standard property. The new buyer can assume the land agreement or buy the house and land together. You keep all house profits.

Q: How long does the closing process take?

A: It operates on a standard 30-day escrow timeline, working seamlessly with title companies and traditional lenders.
