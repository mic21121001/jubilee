2. The Big Idea: Simple & Clear

Over the last ten years, land prices exploded. Today, when you buy a house, half the price tag isn't the building—it's the expensive dirt underneath it. Traditional banks make you borrow money for both, driving up your down payment and monthly mortgage.

The Two-Step Fix

Two-Step Homeownership separates the house from the land[cite: 5]. You buy and own the actual home today with a normal mortgage[cite: 6]. Jubilee covers the land[cite: 6]. That means a much smaller loan, a lower down payment, and a monthly payment that actually fits your budget[cite: 6]. You can buy the land whenever you're ready—or just enjoy owning your home[cite: 6].
